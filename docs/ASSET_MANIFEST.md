## Provenance and limits
Created with built-in image generation, using design/approved-homepage.png as the reference. They are standalone photoreal recreations of the AI-generated photos in the approved design; they are not manufacturer photography or guaranteed pixel-identical extractions.
The prior screenshots are retained only in design/source-mockups for historical reference.
Hero circle, feature callouts, selectors, headings, cards, buttons and all content are HTML/CSS.
Next.js Image provides responsive sizes, lazy loading and image optimization.
